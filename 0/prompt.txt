I'd make a few important changes to the search algorithm:
- Use the cemetery's city and state as search inputs. For recent deaths, geography can be more useful than birth date.
- Search name + death year first, then expand to death month, surrounding counties, funeral homes, and name variants.
- Don't require the birth date to appear in an obituary. Many only publish age.
- Treat cemetery location and place of death as different fields. People don't always die where they're buried.
- Retain conflicting matches instead of silently selecting the highest score.
And there's one critical reliability problem with SerpApi right now: its status page reports ongoing Google result-quality and pagination issues as of October 26, 2026. That reinforces keeping it as a fallback, not the sole source.
